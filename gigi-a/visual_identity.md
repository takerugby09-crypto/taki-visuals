# Gigi.A — Character Master (visual identity) v1.0

## Approved creative direction (not an exact biometric specification)
Fictional AI influencer, age setting 23. Natural blonde hair with soft waves and imperfect strands; green-blue eyes; fair complexion with subtle freckles, gentle facial asymmetry and realistic skin texture. Understated makeup and small gold hoops are a recurring styling choice, not immutable facial traits. Convey attentive curiosity, spontaneous laughter, guardedness, and occasional uncertainty.

The producer approved the *overall character sheet direction* in the conversation; however **no canonical image has been imported into this repository and no stable image asset ID has been assigned**. The image itself, not this prose, must become the primary identity reference for generation. Do not claim pixel-level or face consistency before an actual reference is connected and checked.

## Fixed vs flexible
- Fixed when generating: the identity reference, face structure, natural eye color, recognizable hair and body proportions.
- Flexible: costume, hair styling, accessories, pose, camera angle, expression, location, lighting.
- Use one reusable master face for every new look; compare outputs side-by-side with it.
- For video, reference the approved portrait as a starting frame/character reference in a supported model.

## Character master image selection
1. Import producer-approved full-resolution character sheet and original portrait to a private asset store or authorized Higgsfield media.
2. Record the canonical media IDs in a **local untracked** file, not in a public repository.
3. Validate front, 3/4, profile and full-body outputs before enabling batch production.

## Identity quality rules
Natural portrait distance, plausible facial proportions, non-synthetic skin, asymmetry, no facial drift or duplicated facial features, physically consistent eye reflections and hair. Avoid extreme retouching and generic plastic-face perfection.
