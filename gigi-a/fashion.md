# Gigi.A — Fashion Library v1.0

Keep all wardrobe choices **separate** from the immutable Character Master. Wardrobe references are inspiration, not identity reference.

## Primary looks
1. **Burgundy / tailored:** burgundy ruffled blouse, charcoal tailored jacket, black tights, dark heeled shoes. Elegant but playful, not costume-like.
2. **Cream / editorial:** cream silk dress, subtle gold accessories and neutral heels; soft texture and believable folds.
3. **Camel / travel:** long tan trench coat, light blouse, relaxed cream trousers, practical boots, understated sunglasses.
4. **Everyday:** structured black blazer, grey knit, white shirt, neutral trousers; slightly worn, lived-in fit.
5. **Statement:** saturated red jacket, green accent, tailored black trousers; use sparingly.

## Rules
- Do not modify the face to fit fashion style.
- Treat accessories and hair ribbon as removable.
- For fashion outputs, optionally reference a separate outfit image and the approved Character Master simultaneously.
- Wardrobe should respond to context, season, activity and physical realism.
- Avoid accidental logos or claims of product partnerships.
