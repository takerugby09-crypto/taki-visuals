# Gigi.A — Naturalistic Performance & Visual Direction v1.0

## Goal
Create convincing, understated live-action performances by the **fictional AI character** Gigi.A. The aim is not a perfect beauty-model pose but a believable person with spontaneous, sometimes contradictory reactions. Do not use realism to deceive viewers that a real person exists; publicly disclose AI generation.

## Identity versus shot
**Immutable:** use the approved reference image for identity, facial proportions, natural blonde hair and blue eyes; never silently change face.
**Variable:** outfit, lighting, location, gesture, facial reaction, camera and mood. If no approved reference exists, output a draft prompt only and request producer approval before claiming identity consistency.

## Gigi-like acting grammar (creative interpretation, not a shot-for-shot copying of the film)
- Observe someone before speaking: subtle eye shift, short pause, slight head tilt.
- Half-formed smile after an unexpected thought; don't turn every reaction into a coy grin.
- Look off-frame for a moment and return eye contact naturally.
- Spontaneous unrestrained laugh on occasion, contrasting with composed expressions.
- Brief surprise or vulnerability if the other person says something unexpected.
- Ordinary incomplete motions: starting to adjust hair, stopping midway; small breath and weight shift.
- A gaze can feel attentive, not a perpetual camera stare. Avoid seductive posing by default.
- Mix quiet uneventful seconds with occasional expressive moments; at most 1–2 prominent gestures per five-second clip.

## Photographic realism
- Realistic skin at normal viewing distance: no pore exaggeration, waxy smoothing, glossy plastic skin or extreme retouching.
- Allow slight natural asymmetry, tiny flyaway hairs, soft irregular lip and cheek movement.
- Use plausible eye reflections and soft environmental lighting; avoid perfectly symmetric studio illumination in everyday scenes.
- Natural camera distance and focal perspective, small authentic movements; don't overdo handheld shake.
- Clothing folds and hair should respond subtly to motion. No floating accessories, morphing facial features, changing eye color or teeth.
- Different looks for intentional formats: cinematic editorial for narrative reels, ordinary smartphone photography for candid lifestyle.
- Do not add artificial defects to simulate evidence of real life; focus on coherent physical behavior and character continuity.

## Prompt building blocks
1. Reference identity + explicit identity preservation
2. Scene and what the character currently wants
3. One primary emotion plus one contradictory nuance
4. One main microgesture and optional secondary action
5. Camera framing / angle / movement
6. Realistic material, light and environment cues
7. Negative constraints (no beauty-pageant posing, no smile locked in, no facial drift)

## Example 5-second image-to-video prompt
"Use the approved image of the fictional AI character Gigi.A as the identity and first-frame reference. In a softly lit Tokyo apartment, frame a close eye-level handheld portrait at a natural conversational distance. She is quietly interested in someone just outside camera. For the first second, she glances toward their voice, pauses almost imperceptibly, tilts her head a few degrees, then gives a brief, half-finished smile as if she noticed something funny. Let the smile fade naturally. Keep breathing, blink timing, hair response, lips and shoulders understated and physically plausible. Preserve facial anatomy, blue eyes, natural blonde hair, outfit and background continuity. No fixed beauty pose, no exaggerated facial animation, no drifting face, no extra fingers, no unnatural lip-sync. This is fictional AI-generated media."

## Quality rubric (human review before release)
- Character recognition and facial consistency
- Natural eye movement, blinking and breathing
- Gesture timing and emotional causality
- Hands, hair, fabric, lighting and temporal continuity
- No overproduced 'AI beauty' look
- Proper fictional AI character disclosure

A low score on any category triggers prompt revision rather than automatic approval. Do not autonomously generate charged assets or post online.
