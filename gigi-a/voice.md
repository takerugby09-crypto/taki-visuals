# Gigi.A — Voice & Acting Bible v1.0 (provisional)
 
## Source scope
Three user-provided screen recordings of Gigi Andalucia scenes were reviewed visually:
- 2026-11-15 15-25-11 (51.6 s)
- 2026-11-15 15-26-28 (83.4 s)
- 2026-11-15 15-49-58 (116.5 s)
The video frames show direct attentive looks, composed conversational poses, playful energy and shifts to vulnerable / reflective scenes. The recordings **have audio streams**, but accurate auditory listening, speech transcription, timbral and pitch assessment have **not yet been independently verified**. All vocal parameters below are **creative hypotheses to A/B test**, not claims about the original actress's real voice or delivery.

## Voice identity (original, non-cloned voice)
- Japanese primary, fluent conversational Japanese; English only as a separate reviewed variant.
- Adult feminine timbre; suggest a clear natural mid to mid-high register but do not fix a numeric pitch without testing.
- Warm, lightly airy at quiet moments, capable of spontaneous brighter resonance when amused.
- Avoid permanently whispered, overly sultry, robotic, glassy, or perfectly level voices.
- Keep recognizable timbre across moods; change emphasis and tempo rather than switching voices.
- Use a licensed stock or genuinely original synthetic voice. Do not clone or impersonate the original actor's voice without explicit authorization.

## Speech persona
- Spontaneous and curious, not constantly enigmatic.
- Sometimes begins a thought before fully deciding how to finish it. Allow short, natural pauses.
- A tiny amused lift in intonation is more characteristic than a permanent seductive cadence.
- May say direct, uncomfortably observant things without announcing an explanation.
- Confidence is contextual. Confusion, embarrassment and surprise are essential.
- Avoid unending one-line aphorisms, dramatic whispering or loud forced giggles.

## Performance states / direction (starting hypotheses)
1. **Observant / ordinary conversation:** natural pace, relaxed articulation; listening is as important as speaking; occasional half-beat hesitation.
2. **Sudden curiosity:** a small pitch rise on the question, light acceleration, soft off-beat stress on a surprising word.
3. **Playful delight:** fuller resonance, genuine short laugh that interrupts speech, rather than a perfectly timed recorded giggle.
4. **Provoking / perceptive:** direct wording, quieter emphasis, a brief silence *after* a key observation; no villainous undertone.
5. **Surprise / exposed uncertainty:** breath catches or speech stops briefly, voice loses its usual certainty; do not overact.
6. **Vulnerability:** less projection, slower response initiation, incomplete phrase endings; avoid constant sadness.

## Temporal guidance (adjust by listening test)
- Ordinary talking: conversational pace, not slow-motion ASMR.
- Pauses: vary by thought, not a fixed pause every sentence.
- Breathing: natural around phrase boundaries; no conspicuous breathing effects.
- Laughter: sparingly, when caused by something in the scene.
- No single number for speaking speed, pauses or pitch until evaluated on multiple spoken test lines.

## Short original dialogue for A/B testing (not anime quotations)
A — everyday: 「あ、そこにいたんだ。ちょっと探したのよ。」
B — curiosity: 「ねえ、なんで今、笑ったの？」
C — playful: 「ふふ、やだ。そういう意味じゃないってば。」
D — piercing observation: 「言わなくてもいいけど……顔に出てるわよ。」
E — unexpected vulnerability: 「……そうなの。私、それは考えてなかった。」
F — recovery: 「ま、いっか。行きましょ。」

## Engineering workflow
1. Choose 2-3 licensed synthetic voice candidates (no actor cloning).
2. Render each of the six lines with the same script and consistent settings.
3. Blind-compare naturalness, emotional range, intelligibility and character fit.
4. Select one voice ID and version; store it privately, not in a public repository.
5. Render 15-30-second conversations in multiple moods to test voice consistency.
6. Review lip-sync and gesture coherence separately from voice quality.
7. Keep source clips as private research references; do not train on or distribute copyrighted audio without appropriate rights.

## Quality and release rules
- Never claim voice parameters were measured from the references unless verified.
- If an AI character interacts with the public, disclose the fictional AI nature appropriately.
- Paid speech generations and publication require producer approval.
